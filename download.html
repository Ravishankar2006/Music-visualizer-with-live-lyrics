Apple Music

aaplmusicdownloader.com

Apple Music Downloader lets you paste any Apple Music song, album or playlist URL and download a high‑quality MP3 directly to your device — no account or subscription needed on your end.

1

Open Apple Music (web or app) and find the song you want.

2

Click ⋯ → Share → Copy Link to copy the song URL.

3

Paste the link into aaplmusicdownloader.com and hit Download.

4

Save the MP3, then drag & drop it into the visualizer!

Spotify

spotidownloader.com

SpotiDownloader converts any Spotify track, playlist or album URL into a downloadable MP3. It's fast, free, and works right in the browser — no app installation required.

1

Open Spotify and navigate to the track you love.

2

Click ⋯ → Share → Copy Song Link (or Ctrl+L on desktop).

3

Head to spotidownloader.com, paste the link and click Download.

4

Drop the downloaded MP3 into the Music Visualizer and enjoy!

💡 Tips for the Best Experience

🎶

Use MP3 or FLAC — the visualizer supports MP3, FLAC, WAV, OGG, and M4A. For the richest visuals, higher bitrate files (256–320 kbps) produce the best beat-detection.

📂

Drop multiple files at once — the visualizer accepts a whole queue. Grab your playlist and drag them all in together.

📜

Add synced lyrics — after loading your MP3, click the 🎵 LRC button to load a lyrics file (obtainable from sites like lrclib.net) for a karaoke-style display.

⚖️

Respect copyright — only download music you have the right to use. These tools are intended for personal, offline listening of content you already have access to.
